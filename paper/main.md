## Current claim

The manuscript asks whether PDE residual penalties add predictive value beyond matched smoothness penalties. The locked Burgers holdout supports gains over data-only fitting, but does not establish an advantage over gradient smoothing and favors curvature smoothing. In the reused Allen--Cahn development pool, interval and pointwise PDE penalties have nearly identical field means, with initial-time and derivative-error trade-offs. The paper does not claim a new architecture, formal equivalence, universal superiority, or an untouched Allen--Cahn confirmation.
